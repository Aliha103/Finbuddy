# Design direction: how it was chosen

Sixteen complete design directions were written by independent agents from the same brief (`.arena/task.md`, 11 Oct 2026, Arena run s454129). Four group judges scored them on the Arena rubric (correctness, completeness, specificity, clarity) plus feasibility against the installed stack and ambition; a final judge picked the winner from the four group winners.

**Winner: a004** (recorded verbatim in `DIRECTION.md`). Final ranking: a004, a008, a009, a013.

## Why it won

a004 (the fare card and its gate) is the direction to build because it is the only finalist that clears every binding bar without a workaround. It is honest by construction (owing is ink, never red; red exists only for overdue and destructive actions; sample data wears a chip; the site's only proof is the real split engine running on the page; store buttons say "At launch" and open an honest dialog), and it is genuinely native on both phones (a per-platform table for tabs, sheets versus full-screen dialogs, pickers, feedback and icons, with NativeTabs, iOS 26 bottom accessory and Material FAB rules spelled out). It is buildable today: every API it names exists in the installed expo-router 57, Reanimated 4.5.1, Skia 2.6.2 and Gesture Handler 2.32 types, its five mobile files compile clean under tsc --strict against this repo, every dependency version it pins exists on npm, and it is the one entry that explicitly keeps apps/web out of the npm workspaces so the root's vite ^7.3.0 override cannot break the install. Against the "beyond imagination" goal it is the boldest identity in the pool (Fare Magenta, flat card-stock group cards, Mona Sans tabular readouts) rather than the fourth transit map or the category's green, and its signature moves (Roll to Zero, the Gate, the shared card between list and detail) are one coherent ritual rather than a scatter of effects. a008 is the strongest runner-up with the most product-specific signature (the pairwise-to-simplified arc morph) and flawless sample figures, but it ships a setState-during-render bug in its ThemeProvider, colours debt red, and its world was chosen by four of sixteen entrants. a009 is correct and restrained but admits it is the category hue and would trip on the workspace/vite conflict. a013 is the most novel world but embeds the full app export on the marketing site against PRODUCT.md and quotes hero numbers its own code cannot produce.

## Winner at a glance

- **world.** The transit fare card and its gate: every group is a flat ID-1 card in one of eight line colours, every balance is a gate readout (one exact number in its own currency, never summed), and money actions are confirmed by holding while a ring closes. Sparse, flat, honest; one big number at a time; owing set in ink, never red.
- **signatureInteraction.** Roll to Zero and the Gate: money readouts whose digits roll like a gate display (formatMoneyToParts tokens, Reanimated columns keyed from the right, critically damped and overshoot-clamped), and touch-and-hold for 600 ms on settle/confirm while a Skia ring closes with haptics at start and at the gate, after which the readout rolls to zero. Reduce Motion and screen readers get a crossfade and a two-step Alert instead.
- **palette.** Fare Magenta #C2187A as the one tint (#FF66B8 in dark) on tinted grounds #F5F3F6 / #121016 with ink #1A1420 / #F6F2F7; credit (owed to you) Leaf #1F7A4D / #5FD39A, debit (you owe) is ink, attention amber #9A5B00 / #FFB857, danger #B3261E / #FFB4AB only for overdue, failure and destructive; eight flat line colours per group (magenta, violet, tangerine, marigold, leaf, moss, cocoa, graphite), never per currency; Material dynamic colour off.
- **type.** Mona Sans Bold/SemiBold tabular figures for readouts and titles (@expo-google-fonts/mona-sans@0.4.1 on native, @fontsource-variable/mona-sans@5.3.0 with 110% width on the site); platform face (SF Pro / Roboto) for all body, labels and controls; readouts 40/28/22/17, capped at 1.6x font scale, rows stack at 1.35x.
- **logo.** A magenta ID-1-cornered tile carrying three white bars: one full bar over the same length split into two equal bars, reading as an equals sign (a settled balance) and as a total split fairly. Custom monoline wordmark drawn as paths in currentColor with the i-dot as a magenta tile; complete iOS 1024 icon and Android adaptive foreground/background/monochrome layers, rasterised once with sharp.
- **site.** Single-page Vite 8 / React 19.3 / Tailwind 4 marketing site aliasing the real @finbuddy/shared engine: a full-bleed magenta hero whose primary object is a white Split Card you type into (live resolveSplit, £100 three ways gives 33.34/33.33/33.33, Gate and Roll to Zero on the web via GSAP), a pinned three.js card fan of five group cards (React Three Fiber), a ScrollTrigger-scrubbed multi-currency rail, a Flip/DrawSVG/MotionPath settle diagram, a sample spending chart with an insight and its basis, a plain 'What is true today' section with FAQ, and honest 'App Store / Google Play, At launch' buttons opening a dialog that links to GitHub releases. ThreeUI read and declined with reasons; everything inside gsap.matchMedia for reduced motion.

## Ideas grafted from the runners-up

These are adopted into the build without changing the winner's world.

- **From a008:** The SettleMap morph: draw a group's debts as Skia arcs between member rings in the group's line colour and morph between pairwiseDebts ('All debts') and simplifyDebts ('Fewest payments') from one shared progress value, with amount chips as real Text riding the apex (a008's type-checked SettleMap.tsx, minus its ThemeProvider).
  *Why:* a004's Group detail only lists pairwise debts behind a 'Show direct debts' disclosure; the morph is the single most product-specific signature in the whole pool because it makes simplifyDebts visible and trusted. Drawn on the group's fare card in its line colour it extends a004's world (a line between stations on a card) rather than importing a008's navy map.
- **From a008:** The worked-example discipline: quote only figures the engine produces for createSampleData (owe £57.55 and JP¥21,486, owed US$185.92; Trip plan You→Blake ¥21,486 + Avery→Blake ¥8,541; Dinner club 6 pairwise → 3; ¥9,800/3 = 3,267/3,267/3,266), plus the remainder '+1' tick on the participants who carry the leftover minor unit in the add-expense split preview.
  *Why:* a004's STORY and CardFan copy carry two wrong sample figures; a008 shows the fix is to derive every quoted number from the engine. The remainder tick is the visible proof of largest-remainder allocation and costs only a LinearTransition and one selection haptic.
- **From a008:** Embed the Mona Sans TTFs with the expo-font config plugin (fonts: [...] in app.json) and hold the splash with SplashScreen.preventAutoHideAsync() plus a 1.5 s timeout, while still calling useFonts for Expo Go and the web preview.
  *Why:* a004 loads the readout face at runtime and swaps it in 'on first layout', which is a visible font flash on the hero readout of every cold start; native embedding removes it on devices without changing the fallback path.
- **From a008:** Call Appearance.setColorScheme(preference === 'system' ? 'unspecified' : preference) whenever the in-app appearance override changes (a009 does the same in applyAppearance).
  *Why:* a004's ThemeProvider only recolours its own components, so after choosing Dark in Settings the native tab bar, alerts, sheets, date picker and keyboard would stay light. One line keeps every native piece in agreement with the brand's theme.
- **From a009:** The Exact Bar's implementation for a004's split bar in Exact mode: Pan gesture whose onUpdate converts finger travel to an integer minor-unit delta in a worklet, clamps both neighbours at zero, drives segment widths and per-segment Animated.TextInput labels via useAnimatedProps with a worklet-safe decimal formatter, haptic when a share reaches zero, onChange on release feeding evaluate()/validateSplit, segments as adjustable accessibility elements with increment/decrement actions, and an 8-person cap beyond which entry is numeric only.
  *Why:* a004 describes this interaction in two sentences with no code and leaves the maths implicit; a009's SplitBar.tsx is type-checked against the installed packages, keeps the integers on the UI thread, and gives screen-reader users an equivalent control. It drops into a004's split editor unchanged in colour tokens only.
- **From a009:** Change boot() in src/data/store.ts so first launch with nothing stored sets status 'ready' on an empty snapshot and the index route redirects to /onboarding while profiles.length === 0, keeping resetSampleData as the explicit 'Look around with sample data' action.
  *Why:* a004's onboarding offers 'Start with your own money' versus 'Look around with sample data' but relies on the store's current behaviour of auto-seeding sample data on every first launch; stating the one-branch store change makes the flow real and keeps the sample banner from greeting every new user.
- **From a013:** Set dynamicTypeRamp per type role on iOS Text (largeTitle, title1, title2, headline, body, callout, subheadline, footnote, caption1/2) through a design-system Text primitive, and adopt a013's verified SF Symbol / Material Symbol pair for each of the 15 default categories and the two debt directions.
  *Why:* a004 scales text linearly with allowFontScaling, which is correct on Android but ignores Apple's per-style curves; dynamicTypeRamp is in the installed React Native 0.86 and costs nothing. The icon map is already checked against sf-symbols-typescript and expo-symbols' Android catalogue, saving the build a research pass a004 leaves as 'drawn icons'.
- **From a013:** Give the split-bar Pan gesture activeOffsetX([-6, 6]) and failOffsetY([-12, 12]) so vertical movement stays with the sheet's ScrollView, and add a013's acceptance checklist (every amount via formatMoney with currency, no cross-currency sums, direction by word before colour, split amounts equal engine output on every drag end including zero-decimal currencies, sample never unlabelled, typecheck/lint/test at root, site builds with and without BASE_PATH).
  *Why:* a004 names only activeOffsetX, which lets a horizontal drag inside the add-expense sheet fight the sheet's scroll on Android; the checklist turns a004's contract into verifiable build gates.

## Defects the build must fix

- Correct the sample-data copy to what the engine actually produces: the STORY's 'You're owed £185.92' is US$185.92 (the only GBP position is 'You owe £57.55'), and the CardFan's 'Trip to Japan: ¥21,486 owed to you' is the reverse (you owe Blake ¥21,486). Generate every quoted figure, in the app and on the site, from createSampleData at build time rather than typing it.
- Readout's digit columns set allowFontScaling={false} on the glyphs but never pre-multiply fontSize/lineHeight by the capped font scale, while the non-digit tokens (symbol, separators) still scale. At any Dynamic Type or font-scale setting above 1.0 the digits stay fixed and the symbols grow, so the readout misaligns and the stated 1.6x cap is not implemented. Compute the role size as size * min(fontScale, 1.6) (a009's Ticker pattern) and apply it to every token.
- Readout relies on accessibilityLiveRegion, which is Android-only; on iOS a changed balance is silent unless the caller announces. Make live readouts call AccessibilityInfo.announceForAccessibility with the spoken (currency-name) form when the amount changes, so the settle-up 'rolls to zero' is heard on VoiceOver too.
- The in-app appearance override must call Appearance.setColorScheme so native alerts, sheets, pickers and the tab bar follow the chosen scheme; today ThemeProvider only swaps its own colour tables.
- The split bar in Exact mode is described, not specified: implement it with integer maths on the UI thread (minor-unit deltas, clamped neighbours, labels via useAnimatedProps, release feeds evaluate()/validateSplit), add failOffsetY so the sheet still scrolls, expose increment/decrement accessibility actions, and extend the moved form.ts with the 'adjustment' method the document promises (the preview's SplitMethod currently has only equal, exact, percentage and shares).
- CardFan uses frameloop='demand' and defers the invalidate() wiring to 'the page'; without it the fan never redraws on scroll. Call useThree().invalidate from the ScrollTrigger onUpdate (or switch to frameloop='always' only while the section is pinned), and ship the static SVG fallback it promises for no-WebGL.
- The Expo web export is the team's only preview loop, and a004 draws the Gate ring, the Home sparkline and the Spending charts in Skia; the web build needs CanvasKit loaded via LoadSkiaWeb / WithSkiaWeb before the first Canvas renders (canvaskit-wasm is already in node_modules), with the plain list or static state shown until it is ready.
- Verify on day one, as the document itself demands, that the Mona Sans TTFs in @expo-google-fonts/mona-sans carry tabular figures (tnum) and the currency glyphs the sample uses (£, ¥, €, $, and ₹/₩/₫ for the global claim); if either is missing, readouts fall back to the platform face with fontVariant tabular-nums, which the build should implement as a switch rather than discover on a device.
- Keep apps/web out of the root npm workspaces exactly as written (the root package.json overrides vite to ^7.3.0, which would fight vite 8.3.4), keep netlify.toml's base apps/web, and give the Vite dev server server.fs.allow for ../../packages/shared since the engine alias points outside the site root.
- Wire the motor-accessibility escape hatch the risks section promises: a Settings toggle 'Confirm with a dialog instead of holding' that routes HoldToConfirm to the same two-step Alert path used for screen readers and Reduce Motion, persisted app-locally.
- Declare expo-symbols and expo-glass-effect in apps/mobile/package.json (they are present only transitively via expo-router) and keep the stated fallbacks: JS Tabs if unstable-native-tabs changes, a filled magenta capsule where isLiquidGlassAvailable() is false.

## Group scores

- a001: 49 (fatal). Friendship-bracelet floss on each platform's own ground: every person is a coloured strand (you are always fuchsia), an expense is a woven band whose knots you pull to re-split, settling ties a knot and debts are never red; the site is a marigold fridge door with a tuggable ThreeUI cloth bill that comes down peg by peg.
- a002: 87. Beads on a Rail: the abacus reduced to a rail (the total) and beads (people); the Split Rail is a Skia canvas where dragging a bead trades exact minor units between neighbours, balances are LedgerRoll digits that roll, settling untangles pairwise strands into the simplifyDebts plan, and the site hero is two rails that merge into one carrying the live engine.
- a003: 87.5. Route Map: people are stations, each currency is its own coloured line that never merges with another, the Rail divides an expense into exact segments with draggable station knobs, and 'Fewer Lines' trims the pairwise tangle into the simplifyDebts plan; the site is one yellow journey line that draws as you scroll, each section a stop.
- a004: 91. The transit fare card and its gate: each group is a flat card-stock card, every balance is a gate readout whose digits Roll to Zero, and money actions are confirmed by holding while a Skia ring closes (the Gate); owing is set in ink, never red, and the site hero is the real split engine presented as a Split Card you type into.
- a005: 76.5. A scorepad/chalkboard world (baize shell, chalk marks, one amber signal, Barlow Semi Condensed numerals) whose signature move is 'Strike it level': drag a debt row and a Skia chalk line draws under the finger, recording a pending payment.
- a006: 71.5. A transit-cartography world (paper/ink grounds, eight route-line colours, Archivo) where each currency is its own line and people are stations; the signature move is dragging your marker along the line to a friend's station to settle.
- a007: 59.5. A chalk-and-ink shell with a single marigold signal where every amount is tinted by its ISO currency (OKLCH hue per code); the signature move is the Split Bar, one bar cut into exact largest-remainder shares with draggable boundaries.
- a008: 87. A night-transit-map world (navy and map white, one 'you' yellow, eight line colours, Big Shoulders numerals) whose signature move is 'Fewest payments': a Skia settle map that morphs a group's pairwise debts into the simplified plan.
- a009: 88. The Board: a sage-and-fern-green counting board where every currency sits on its own rolling-digit row, and the signature move is the Exact Bar, a draggable integer-exact split bar with haptics at zero.
- a010: 83. Postmark: postal-yellow stamp tiles on white paper (black album page at night) where the signature move is 'Stamp it', a 550 ms press-and-hold that lands a procedural Skia postmark to settle a debt.
- a011: 67. The counting frame: lacquer, bone and one persimmon bead, where balances owed sit on a soroban beam and balances you owe hang from it, and settling is a bead slid 85 percent across a track.
- a012: 82. The departures board: a deep-green board of white split-flap digit tiles and status words (DUE, PENDING, SETTLED, SAMPLE), where any amount or word that changes because of the person's own action flips like a split-flap tile.
- a013: 85. The Counting Frame: people are beads and every currency is its own rod, with the Split Rod (drag a bead in whole-share steps, amounts from allocateMinor) as the signature move and a three.js bead field that scroll-settles 11 debts into 3 payments as the site hero.
- a014: 81.5. The transit line diagram: balances drawn as a route map with you as the interchange ring, one line per group per currency, settled segments retracting, and a site hero that is a working sentence-form split engine driving an SVG map.
- a015: 85. International wayfinding: signal-yellow sign plates and ink, balances drawn on a dark Board as coded coloured routes with 45-degree bends, and 'Settle the line', a long-press pull along the route with haptic detents that records a payment (button always available), with a site route map that Flips pairwise debts into the simplifyDebts plan on scroll.
- a016: 83. Departures: a split-flap board world where amounts that change flip cell by cell from the cents up (FlipAmount from formatMoneyToParts), native shells underneath, and a site whose flap board narrates the real app running in a pinned iframe.
